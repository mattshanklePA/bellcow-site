Support

Need a hand?

Email the commissioner. A real person reads every message, usually within a day.

commissioner@bellcowhq.com

Can't sign in

Use the reset link on the sign-in screen. If the email doesn't arrive in a few minutes, check spam, then email us from the address on your account.

Something looks wrong in my league

Tell your league's commissioner first; they can fix most settings, names and scores. If they can't, email us with your league name and a screenshot.

Report a problem or a person

Email us with the league name, the person or post, and a screenshot if you have one. We look at every report.

Delete my account

Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it and your personal data. See the privacy policy for what that covers.
